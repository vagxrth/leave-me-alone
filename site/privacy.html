Leave Me Alone

Privacy

Leave Me Alone exists to stop companies from quietly collecting your details. It doesn’t collect any itself.

Last updated 30 September 2026.

This website

  • It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or tracking pixels.
  • Its fonts, images and code are served from this site. Nothing loads from other companies’ servers.
  • “Check this browser” loads a small page from our second address, lma-check.vercel.app, and asks your browser one question: can a site use cookies from a different site? It runs only when you press the button. The answer stays on the page; it isn’t sent or saved anywhere.

The Chrome extension

The extension works entirely inside your browser. It has no server, makes no network requests of its own, and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

  • It stores two settings on your computer: whether to forget GoKwik’s data each time Chrome starts, and whether you’ve seen the welcome note.
  • When you pause it on a store, it adds a rule in your browser that lets GoKwik load on that store. Resuming removes the rule.

Why it asks for each permission:

  • Block content on any page (declarativeNetRequest): Chrome blocks GoKwik’s tracking requests using a fixed list of rules. The extension itself never sees the requests.
  • activeTab and scripting: When you open the extension, it checks whether the current page uses GoKwik and counts what was blocked. When you press “Forget my GoKwik data”, it removes the items GoKwik saved on that store. It can only do this on the tab you’re looking at, and only after you click.
  • browsingData: Deletes the cookies and saved data belonging to GoKwik’s own addresses.
  • storage: Keeps the two settings above.
