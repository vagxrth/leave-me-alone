You only looked. So how did they get your number?

Many Indian online stores run the same hidden code from GoKwik, a checkout company. If you ever typed your phone number into one of them, the others can recognise you and send you WhatsApp messages about what you looked at.

Fix it on your phone Get the Chrome extension

How a store you never told gets your number

  1. You type your number on one store

    At checkout, for an OTP login, or in a pop-up offering a discount, on any store that uses GoKwik. GoKwik says it’s “Trusted by 20,000+ leading D2C brands”.

  2. GoKwik remembers your browser

    It saves an ID in a year-long cookie on its own domain. Its hidden code on every other GoKwik store can read that cookie.

  3. Another store recognises you

    You open a different GoKwik store and type nothing. GoKwik can tell it who you are and which products you looked at. Then a WhatsApp message can follow.

What we found

  • On 26 September 2026 we opened six stores in a fresh browser: Mamaearth, boAt, Bombay Shaving Company, mCaffeine, The Man Company and Plum. The same GoKwik ID followed us into all six, and GoKwik counted each visit.
  • 171 of the 225 GoKwik stores we checked run this identity code, which GoKwik calls KwikPass.
  • All 158 KwikPass stores whose settings we could read use GoKwik’s “opt-out” consent mode. In GoKwik’s code, that mode starts the “Notify me for orders, updates & offers” box already ticked.

“Identify up to 30% visitors, even those who don’t sign up. Track them across visits.”

GoKwik’s KwikPass product page

Fix it in a minute

Pick the device you shop on. Checkout keeps working; you’ll just type your number yourself when you buy.

On Android, in Chrome

  1. Block third-party cookies

    This is the main fix. It stops GoKwik’s cookie ID from following you between stores. We tested it: the six stores that shared one GoKwik ID each got a different one, and checkout still worked.

    Chrome menu ⋮›Settings›Privacy and security›Third-party cookies›Block third-party cookies

  2. Delete what GoKwik already saved

    This removes the ID GoKwik saved. Then repeat it for each store that already messages you, searching for the store’s name. That logs you out of the store.

    Chrome menu ⋮›Settings›Site settings›All sites›Search “gokwik”›Clear & reset

  3. Check it worked

    This asks your browser one question: can a site read cookies from a different site? It never contacts GoKwik or any store.

Extra protection for DNS blocker users

If you use a DNS blocker such as NextDNS or AdGuard DNS, add prd-gfp.gokwik.co (device fingerprinting) and sdk.gokwik.co (browsing reports).

Don’t block pdp.gokwik.co or gkx.gokwik.co. Checkout needs them.

On iPhone, in Safari

  1. Cookies are already covered

    Safari keeps each site’s cookies separate, so GoKwik’s cookie ID can’t follow you between stores.

  2. Check fingerprint protection is on

    It makes your phone harder to recognise without cookies. Choose All Browsing.

    Settings›Apps›Safari›Advanced›Advanced Tracking and Fingerprinting Protection›All Browsing

  3. Delete what GoKwik already saved

    Settings›Apps›Safari›Advanced›Website Data›Search “gokwik”›Swipe left, Delete

  4. Check it worked

    This asks your browser one question: can a site read cookies from a different site? It never contacts GoKwik or any store.

On a computer, in Chrome, Edge or Brave

  1. Install the Leave Me Alone extension

    It blocks GoKwik’s identity code, flags stores that use it, and leaves checkout working. Until it’s listed in the Chrome Web Store, install it from GitHub:

    Download from GitHub

    Unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder inside.

  2. Block third-party cookies too

    Settings›Privacy and security›Third-party cookies›Block third-party cookies

  3. Forget what GoKwik saved

    Open the extension and click Forget my GoKwik data, once on each store that already messages you.

  4. Check it worked

    This asks your browser one question: can a site read cookies from a different site? It never contacts GoKwik or any store.

Stop the messages you already get

The fixes above stop new stores recognising you. Stores that already have your number keep it, so deal with them directly.

  1. Tap “Not interested”, then “Stop”

    WhatsApp shows these under a business’s offers and announcements. Stop turns off that business’s marketing messages; order updates still arrive.

  2. Block and report

    Tap the business’s name at the top of the chat, then Block and Report. Reports lower the business’s standing with WhatsApp, which can limit its marketing messages.

  3. Ask them to delete your number

    Send one of the emails below. Online stores must acknowledge a complaint within 48 hours.

To the store

Send it to the store’s support or grievance email, usually listed on its Contact or Privacy page.

Subject: Unsolicited WhatsApp message: where did you get my number?

Hello,

On [date] I received a WhatsApp marketing message from your business on [your number]. I never gave you my number or agreed to WhatsApp messages. I had only browsed your website.

Please:
1. Tell me how and from whom you got my number (for example, GoKwik KwikPass or another partner).
2. Stop all WhatsApp, SMS and email marketing to me.
3. Delete my personal data from your systems and marketing tools, and ask your service providers to do the same.

Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, please acknowledge this complaint within 48 hours.

Regards,
[your name]

To GoKwik

Send it to GoKwik’s grievance officer: grievance-officer@gokwik.co

Subject: Delete my data from the KwikPass / GoKwik shopper network

Hello,

My mobile number is [your number]. I have received WhatsApp marketing from brands such as [brand names] after only browsing their websites. I never gave these brands my number or agreed to their messages. These stores run GoKwik KwikPass.

Please:
1. Tell me what personal data GoKwik holds linked to my number, including device and fingerprint identifiers, and which merchants received my number through KwikPass.
2. Delete my number and every linked identifier from your identity and single sign-on systems, and stop sharing my number with merchants I haven’t bought from.
3. Withdraw any marketing consent recorded for me, and confirm that no GoKwik service will send me marketing.

Your grievance policy commits to acknowledging within 24 hours and responding within 15 days.

Regards,
[your name]

Is this allowed?

If you never gave a store your number, WhatsApp’s own rules say it shouldn’t message you. Here’s where things stand.

WhatsApp’s rules

A business may only message you if you gave it your mobile number and agreed to hear from it on WhatsApp. It must also respect your block or opt-out.

WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy

India’s data law

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, consent must be free, specific, informed and given by a clear affirmative action. Its main rules apply from 14 May 2027.

DPDP Rules, 2025

Consumer complaints

Online stores must have a grievance officer who acknowledges complaints within 48 hours. If that fails, call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915.

National Consumer Helpline

This is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

Will checkout still work?

Yes. With third-party cookies blocked, and separately with the extension, GoKwik’s checkout opened and asked for a phone number on every store we tried, including Mamaearth and boAt. When you buy, you type your number yourself. One thing changes with the extension: a store’s GoKwik login button may not open, because GoKwik runs that login inside the part we block. You can log in at checkout, or pause the extension for that store.

Will this stop every marketing message?

No. It stops stores recognising you through GoKwik. Stores that already have your number keep it; use the steps under “Stop the messages you already get”.

Is it only GoKwik?

GoKwik is the network we found and tested. Other checkout companies may work differently, and we haven’t tested them yet.

Does this site collect anything about me?

No. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, and the extension sends nothing anywhere. The privacy page has the details.
